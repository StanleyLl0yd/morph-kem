**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Status:** source-first, analytic-only **negative-control / statistics theorem**. **GLOBAL NO NOMINATION; H-A/H-B/H-C HOLD.** Not a new F0-K/N1 number, implemented attack, empirical corpus, novel independent foundation, crypto assumption, proof of average-case hardness, OWF/PQ/PKE/KEM or trapdoor.

**Do not re-announce an existing result:** the polynomial inversion theorem when public uniform planted alternating forms span their *entire* compatible form space, with **arbitrary codimension**, was already proved in [#208, comment 6215130941](https://github.com/StanleyLl0yd/morph-kem/issues/208#issuecomment-6215130941) on 2026-10-16. It computes the common annihilator in \(\Lambda^2V\) and recovers the planted plane by contractions. Its exact source spanning probability is likewise already recorded there. The **new scoped result in this file** is an exact *first- and second-moment* calculation for the number of projective decomposable bivectors in that public annihilator, under both planted and null laws. The existence of this count does **not** make it polynomially computable on arbitrary inputs.

## 1. Honest and null source, full original relation

Let \(K=\mathbb F_q\), \(q\ge2\), \(V=K^n\), \(2\le d<n\), and \(c=n-d\ge1\). Denote \(N=\binom n2\) and \(h=\binom d2\). Use the canonical perfect evaluation pairing
\[
(\Lambda^2V)^*\cong\Lambda^2 V^*,
\qquad \langle A,v\wedge w\rangle=A(v,w),
\]
including **characteristic two**: all alternating matrices must have zero diagonal.

- **Planted source \(Q_{\rm pl}\):** sample **any** d-dimensional \(H\le V\) from a specified unconditional polynomial-time honest source. Conditional on H, independently sample m uniform alternating forms from \(W_H=\{A\in\Lambda^2V^*: A|_{H\times H}=0\}=(\Lambda^2H)^\perp\).
- **Null source \(Q_0\):** independently sample m unrestricted uniform alternating forms on V. There is no guaranteed common totally-isotropic d-space.

The complete original any-witness verifier accepts any \(n\times d\) full-rank Y such that \(Y^TA_iY=0\) for **every public i**; the planted law always contains its planted witness, and no generated instance is filtered for its rank or count.

From the actual full public tuple compute the public linear subspace
\[
K_P=\{\omega\in\Lambda^2V:
       \langle A_i,\omega\rangle=0 \text{ for every }i\}.
\]
Under \(Q_{\rm pl}\), \(\Lambda^2H\subseteq K_P\) **on every sample**. Let \(X(P)\) be the number of **projective one-dimensional subspaces in \(K_P\) generated by nonzero decomposable bivectors**. These are in bijection with the set of all 2-dimensional subspaces \(J\le V\) satisfying \(A_i|_{\Lambda^2J}=0\) for all i. Define the Gaussian-binomial counts
\[
T={n\brack2}_q=|\operatorname{Gr}(2,V)|,\quad
S={d\brack2}_q=|\operatorname{Gr}(2,H)|,\quad
p=q^{-m}.
\]
No assertion is made that X can be evaluated polynomially from the public linear space \(K_P\).

## 2. Null law: exact first moment and variance

**Theorem 1.** Under the full unconditioned \(Q_0\),
\[
\boxed{\quad\mathbb E_0[X]=Tp,\qquad
       \operatorname{Var}_0(X)=Tp(1-p).\quad}
\]

**Proof.** Each 2-plane \(J\) has a nonzero projective decomposable bivector \([\omega_J]\); for any fixed J, each uniform public alternating form evaluates to an independent uniform scalar on its nonzero \(\omega_J\), so \(\Pr[J\text{ survives}]=q^{-m}=p\). If \(J\ne J'\), their projective vectors \([\omega_J]\ne[\omega_{J'}]\) in \(\mathbf P(\Lambda^2V)\) are not scalar multiples, hence \(\omega_J,\omega_{J'}\) are linearly independent. A uniform form's evaluations on the two are independent uniform field elements; across m forms, **their two survival indicators are pairwise independent**. Summing T identical Bernoulli indicators gives the expectation and variance exactly. *Pairwise* is important: no assertion of mutual independence of all T indicators or of a binomial distribution for X is justified.

## 3. Planted law: exact expectation and the entire covariance structure

Put \(U=\Lambda^2H\le\Lambda^2V\). Under the fixed-H planted source, every projective decomposable \([\omega]\in\mathbf P(U)\) survives with probability **one**. For any other projective decomposable \([\omega]\notin\mathbf P(U)\), evaluation of independently uniform elements of \(U^\perp\) on its **nonzero** quotient class in \((\Lambda^2V)/U\) is uniform, so its survival probability is exactly \(p\). Hence, for **every** fixed H,
\[
\boxed{\quad\mathbb E_{\rm pl}[X\mid H]=
 S+(T-S)p.\quad}
\]
In particular the same formula holds under **any unconditional honest H-source** (uniform or not), with no postselection.

For exact variance, define for every projective quotient direction
\[
\ell\in\mathbf P((\Lambda^2V)/U)
\]
the fibre size \(F_\ell\): the number of **decomposable projective** \([\omega]\notin\mathbf P(U)\) whose projection is the projective line \(\ell\). Within one fibre, the survival events are **identical**; for two distinct quotient directions, they are **pairwise independent** because the quotient vectors are linearly independent. Therefore, conditional on any fixed H,
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\rm pl}(X\mid H)
=p(1-p)\sum_\ell F_\ell^2.
\]
The fibre-size distribution has a simple **exact classification**; write \(V=H\oplus C\) for proof only, \(\dim C=c\):

1. If \(J\le H\), then \([\Lambda^2J]\subseteq\mathbf P(U)\). Exactly \(S={d\brack2}_q\) such 2-planes are deterministic and do not enter the variance.
2. If \(\dim(J\cap H)=1\), the projected bivector is a decomposable rank-one direction in \(H\otimes C\subseteq(\Lambda^2V)/U\). Such directions are indexed by a pair of projective lines \(([h],[\bar v])\in\mathbf P(H)\times\mathbf P(C)\), giving exactly \({d\brack1}_q{c\brack1}_q\) **distinct quotient lines**. For each pair, the possible 2-planes \(J=\operatorname{span}(h,v)\) with the fixed transverse quotient line \(\bar v\) are obtained by varying \(v\) by arbitrary elements of H modulo the line \(\langle h\rangle\): precisely \(q^{d-1}\) different J have the **same quotient direction**. Thus \(F_\ell=q^{d-1}\) for each such quotient line.
3. If \(J\cap H=0\), projection onto C identifies J with a 2-plane \(P\le C\) and J is the **graph** of a linear map \(f:P\to H\). There are \({c\brack2}_q\,q^{2d}\) such J. Their bivectors have nonzero \(\Lambda^2 C\) component, and **distinct** \((P,f)\) yield **different projective quotient directions**: equality of the \(\Lambda^2C\) projective part fixes P and its normalization; equality of its \(H\otimes C\) part then fixes f. So each corresponding quotient line has \(F_\ell=1\).

These cases are disjoint, and also give the exact Gaussian-binomial/Vandermonde identity
\[
{n\brack2}_q=
{d\brack2}_q+
q^{d-1}{d\brack1}_q{c\brack1}_q+
q^{2d}{c\brack2}_q.
\]

**Theorem 2.** For **every fixed planted H**, and therefore under **every unconditional honest H-source** (the conditional mean is constant in H, so the law of total variance adds no term),
\[
\boxed{\quad
\mathbb E_{\rm pl}[X]=S+(T-S)p,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\rm pl}(X)=
p(1-p)\left(
q^{2d-2}{d\brack1}_q{c\brack1}_q+
q^{2d}{c\brack2}_q
\right).\quad}
\]
All formulas hold for *every* finite field, including characteristic two; no assumption that d or c is fixed is needed. The proof gives the *complete pairwise covariance criterion* (same projective quotient line gives covariance \(p(1-p)\), distinct directions zero), not merely an asymptotic estimate.

## 4. Exact mean shift and the sparse-source scaling boundary

Subtracting Theorem 1 from Theorem 2,
\[
\boxed{\quad
\mathbb E_{\rm pl}[X]-\mathbb E_0[X]
=S(1-p),\qquad
\frac{\mathbb E_{\rm pl}[X]-\mathbb E_0[X]}
{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}_0(X)}}
=\frac{S(1-p)}{\sqrt{Tp(1-p)}}.\quad}
\]

For any fixed field q, when \(d\) grows, \(T=\Theta(q^{2(n-2)})\) and \(S=\Theta(q^{2(d-2)})\), with constants depending only on q. In the externally studied sparse-m range \(m=\lceil n/\log n\rceil\), \(d=\lceil n/C\rceil\), constant \(C\ge3\), \(p=q^{-m}\), so
\[
\frac{\mathbb E_{\rm pl}[X]-\mathbb E_0[X]}
{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}_0(X)}}
=q^{2d-n+m/2+O(1)}
=q^{-\Omega(n)}.
\]
Thus, **the planted contribution to the mean of this particular count is exponentially smaller than the null standard deviation** in that regime. Moreover the exact planted variance above is of the **same leading exponential order** as the null variance when c grows linearly and d<c; the leading transverse-graph term has \(q^{2d}{c\brack2}_q=\Theta(q^{2n-4})\).

**Essential caution:** comparing two moments does **not** prove TV indistinguishability of X, the absence of a successful detector based on X, computational intractability of finding these 2-planes, or hardness of the original any-witness inversion. The full planted/null tensor laws can have large TV even when this moment-scale signal is small. We have **no polynomial X-counter** in the hard parameter range; this is an information-theoretic count analysis, not an algorithm.
