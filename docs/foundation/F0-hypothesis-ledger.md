## Decision method

For each family, first define an unconditional polynomial sampler of \((P,s)\) and an exact polynomial verifier \(V(P,y)\) including the entire admissible domain, and state \(E(P)=\{y:V(P,y)=1\}\). Then independently attempt public sections, algebraic quotients/gauge removal, mature problem reductions, finite-width CSP DP, degree/character-sum leakage, and arbitrary accepted-witness search. Only afterward consider any pre-registered bounded computation.

**Five independent gates** (failure at any gate stops before benchmarks):

1. **Computable interface:** finite, polynomial public bitlength, unconditional polynomial sampler, correct planted verifier and explicit honest distribution. No hidden exponentially expensive counting.
2. **Public section:** a deterministic or randomized polynomial attacker that outputs *any* accepted witness on the honest source distribution kills the proposed inversion claim, regardless of fibre cardinality.
3. **Known mathematical representation:** cite and attempt reductions to canonical normal forms, lattice/linear algebra, group actions, CSP, tomography, graph/hypergraph decomposition, rewriting problems, sheaf cohomology and existing local cryptographic assumptions.
4. **Distributional burden:** absent a public inverter, state the unconditional image distribution and compute/estimate attacks against it. NP-hard worst-case slices, failed timeouts and many planted solutions do not pass.
5. **Positive MORPH-R0 Field 15:** name a **mathematically distinct** relation with positive independent evidence beyond mature assumptions. "I could not find a paper" and "two familiar hard objects were coupled" do **not** pass.

States: **REJECT** (explicit public section or fatal gate), **HOLD** (major undefined/mature/hardness gap, not eligible for code), **READY FOR COMPLETE 15-FIELD REVIEW** (all five pre-source gates supported, still no nomination), and **NO NOMINATION** (global). There is currently **no** ready family.

## H-A: coupled observations with incompatible public quotients

**Structural hypothesis, not a cryptographic assumption.** Let \(X_\lambda\) be a public efficiently recognizable finite object domain; draw \(x\leftarrow D_\lambda\) with explicit unconditional polynomial sampling and public presentation data \(\alpha,\beta\). Publish \(P=(\alpha,\beta,f_\alpha(x),g_\beta(x))\), where both f and g are polynomially computable and have polynomial output size. Define \(V(P,y)=[y\in X_\lambda\land f_\alpha(y)=P_f\land g_\beta(y)=P_g]\). **Any joint witness wins**. Conjectured *research question*: can a natural new coupled invariant family avoid a public common representative even though each marginal has a public section?

**Why coupling alone is not a result:** [F0-K2](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#4-f0-k2-stacking-polynomially-many-public-linear-views-remains-linear) proves that any number of public finite-field linear views still invert by stacked Gaussian elimination. Earlier [N1-51](https://github.com/StanleyLl0yd/morph-kem/issues/196#issuecomment-6168144976) coupled two fixed-k spectra, yet a public frontier DP inverted all measured profiles in the preregistered tiny protocol. [F0-K4a](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#8-f0-k4a-two-separately-canonicalizable-nonabelian-gauge-views-still-have-a-joint-public-section) shows that **two different public nonabelian gauge views** still admit a trivial union-graph joint section; [F0-K5](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#9-f0-k5-universality-trap---two-trivially-invertible-nonlinear-views-may-encode-any-existing-inverse-task) proves the complementary warning: two independently easy inverse maps can encode arbitrary preexisting h-inversion, so apparent joint hardness alone has no novel mathematical content. [F0-K6](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#10-f0-k6-coupled-frobenius-polynomial-observations-with-a-public-shared-normal-form-remain-linear) excludes all publicly represented Frobenius-polynomial views with a common reversible coordinate normalization: high polynomial degree need not exceed base-field linear algebra. [F0-K7](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#11-f0-k7-fully-satisfiable-permutation-constraints-on-an-explicitly-enumerable-alphabet) also rejects polynomial-alphabet fully satisfiable permutation edge constraints, including overlapping nonlinear bijective views, by root enumeration and full edge verification. [F0-K8](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#12-f0-k8-public-affine-parametrization-of-one-entire-fibre-kills-nonlinear-two-view-inversion) rejects a pair if one complete marginal fibre has a public bijective affine parameterization and the other view is affine **on that fibre**. Polynomial interpolation, multi-view tomography, phaseless recovery, paired group-orbit normalization and generic planted CSP are established neighboring problems.

**Attack-first falsifiers:** (i) exhibit \(s(P)\) accepted simultaneously by f and g, possibly after a publicly computable gauge/canonical normal form; (ii) show linear/rational reconstruction, low-rank lift, simple count/entropy leakage or small fixed seed basis; (iii) formulate exact joint verifier as CSP and inspect primal/hypertree width, local predicate polymorphisms, relaxation and SAT/SMT/MILP; (iv) exhibit a second public sampler matching the accepted image; (v) show that Field 15 collapses to a mature joint inverse problem. Any succeeds => REJECT, not "increase dimension".

**Matched controls to preregister only after Field 15 passes:** same honest x/distribution with a publicly aligned second view, same number of constraints with independent public coordinate layout, and a weak single-view public section. Charge all work against original V and report public-instance bitlength. **No runs have been made.**

**[Residual-inversion core lemma](F0-residual-core-and-acyclic-join.md#2-h-a-public-full-fibre-chart-exposes-an-exactly-equivalent-residual-inversion-problem):** when a first-view fibre has a complete efficiently invertible public chart, the exact joint any-witness inversion problem is distribution-preserving equivalent to inversion of the second view on that fibre. This is a change of variables, not a hardness theorem; the independent Field-15 claim must concern that residual relation.

**[Single alternating-form full-slice screen](F0-one-alternating-form-rank-screen.md):** when an external TI observation has only one public alternating form, symplectic elimination constructs a full-original-verifier witness at **every feasible dimension**, not merely the general multi-slice small-d bound. For the separate ideal uniform planted law, rank is an exact sufficient statistic for detection. Neither statement extends to the multi-slice tensor case or supplies Field 15 originality.

**[Regular alternating-pencil screen](F0-regular-alternating-pencil-public-section.md):** when the publicly matched alternating slices span at most two dimensions and an original-field nonsingular linear combination exists (even n), a polynomial cyclic symplectic algorithm builds a common Lagrangian and passes **every** original slice. Two independent uniform conditioned planted slices give an explicit constant lower bound on the full-honest-law attack success, with exact binary-field strict-PPT sampling; this does **not** extend to ≥3 independent slices or establish new mathematics.

**[Universal multislice hyperplane gate](F0-multislice-isotropic-hyperplane-linearization.md):** for the TI rank-`d=n−1` target, **any number** of public alternating slices collapses to Gaussian elimination on `α∧A_i=0` over **every finite field**. The public linear-kernel dimension also gives the exact ideal uniform hyperplane planted-vs-null likelihood and a polynomial near-perfect high-n detector. This does **not** infer anything comparable for the established planted-TI target `d≈n/C` with `C≥3`, where higher-codimension Plücker constraints remain.

**[Fixed-codimension exterior-kernel screen](F0-fixed-codimension-wedge-kernel-section.md):** for **fixed** `c=n−d`, if the publicly computed wedge map has a one-dimensional kernel, the guaranteed planted decomposable vector yields an exact public section. For a **fully specified** conditional-uniform source and `m≥D=binom(n,2)−binom(d,2)`, observing a full basis of the vanishing-form space happens with exact full-source probability `∏(j=0..D−1)(1−q^(j−m))` and forces this kernel condition. The strict PPT binary-field fallback source remains unconditioned. This does not touch general high-c or sparse-m planted tensors.

**[Two-slice support-intersection attack](F0-two-slice-public-row-support-intersection.md):** for conditional-uniform planted alternating forms, `d=n−c≥2c`, two independently uniform public slices yield the exact full-honest-law public-intersection inverter success probability `∏(j=0..2c−1)(1−q^(j−d))` using **only Gaussian elimination**, independent of fixed-c exterior lifting. This includes strictly bounded exact binary-field generation and does **not** address the external conjectural `d≈n/C` (`C≥3`) regime.

**[Adaptive public image-support screen](F0-adaptive-multislice-support-intersection.md):** even if many planted alternating slices have rank below `2c`, the attacker may publicly keep only rank-`2c` images for reconstructing the annihilator while checking *all* original slices. For any source with independent conditional-uniform slices and `d>c`, classical subspace-lattice Möbius inversion gives the exact success probability, and an elementary union bound gives success approaching one for polynomial `m=O(d log q)` (with explicit error parameter). The original honest distribution is never filtered; `d=c` or `d<c` is outside this sufficient attack boundary.

**[Binary-field Plücker kernel-plane no-go](F0-plucker-two-dimensional-kernel-section.md):** for **fixed codimension c** and `GF(2^e)`, even a *two-dimensional* public wedge kernel supports deterministic polynomial full-original-verifier inversion. Its projective line either lies entirely in the Grassmannian or meets one nonzero quadratic Plücker equation in at most two explicitly solvable points. This is a **pointwise** theorem; it provides no source-law frequency estimate and does not solve large wedge kernels or `c=Θ(n)`.

**[Sparse-source rank-profile and high-corank null controls](F0-sparse-planted-ti-rank-profile-minrank-screen.md):** in `d≈n/C,C>=3,m≈n/log n`, full **individual public slice rank vector** has negligible planted-vs-null TV. The event that *any* public linear combination has corank `>=ceil(2 sqrt(m))+2` is likewise negligible on both laws. These established random-matrix bounds are narrowly scoped **anti-overclaim** results, not a solution of optimally selected lower-threshold MinRank or any-witness TI inversion.

**[Exact dual-kernel bivector-moment null control](F0-dual-bivector-kernel-exact-source-moments.md):** for the full **unconditioned planted vs unrestricted null alternating-form laws**, the expectation and **variance** of the number of projective decomposable 2-vectors in the public annihilator are explicit, including all pairwise covariance fibres outside the planted wedge square. At `d≈n/C,C≥3,m≈n/logn`, the planted mean shift is exponentially smaller than the null standard deviation, which is **not** a proof of TV closeness, easy inversion or computational hardness. The **arbitrary-c dense full-span inverter** was already established in [issue #208 comment 6215130941](https://github.com/StanleyLl0yd/morph-kem/issues/208#issuecomment-6215130941); this new note does not claim it as independent work.

**[Joint precommitted local-view TV via span](F0-planted-ti-precommitted-multiview-span-tv.md):** several jointly GL-invariant randomly selected subspaces can be handled by their total span, allowing a bound on even arbitrarily processed **joint** restrictions when span dimension is below the planted codimension. A chosen carrier correlated with the published tensor is outside scope; no Field-15 novelty or full-data inversion conclusion.

**[Precommitted random restricted-tensor local TV](F0-planted-ti-random-restriction-exact-tv.md):** the full restricted m-tuple on one random t-plane is exactly null-like when T cap H has dimension <=1; Gaussian incidence gives exponentially small real-view TV for t<=c-epsilon*n, even for unbounded processing of that restricted view. This leaves **full public tensor**, public-dependent adaptive subspaces, joint probes and any-witness inversion unresolved; not Field-15 originality evidence.

**[Planted third moment via quotient-line pencils](F0-dual-bivector-planted-third-central-moment.md):** all supported quotient lines are classified into four explicit finite-geometric types, giving an exact planted third central moment and relative agreement with the null third moment in the fixed-q sparse d<c regime. This strengthens a low-order statistical **negative control**, not Field-15 originality, general distributional indistinguishability or a polynomial full-verifier section.

**[Third-order Plücker-line null dependence](F0-dual-bivector-null-third-central-moment.md):** exact null third central moment of Z2 adds a positive projective-line incidence term missing from any naive binomial approximation; at fixed q, m=o(n), the standardized skew is Theta(q^(-m/2)). Does not give planted skew, Gaussianity, total-variation closeness or a polynomial counter, and does not improve H-A originality.

**[Exact r=3 null-orthogonal contrast screen](F0-isotropic-three-plane-orthogonal-contrast.md):** subtracting the full null linear projection of Z3 onto Z2 cancels the leading planted t=2 mean contribution exactly and yields explicit finite q,n,d,m null variance and planted mean shift. In one-slice cases the contrast is pointwise constant; in the fixed-q sparse d<c regime its standardized mean is exponentially small. This concerns only abstract counts; it is neither TV closeness nor an efficient public detection/inversion theorem.

**[Joint fixed-r isotropic-count null fluctuations](F0-isotropic-subspace-mixed-covariance-rank-one.md):** for all fixed r,k the null cross covariance is an exact Grassmann-incidence sum. At m=o(n), the finite-vector null covariance is asymptotically rank-one along the r=2 fluctuations, while mixed planted covariance is only given by an exact (exponential) quotient-rank sum. This is no TV/detection-hardness theorem; subleading contrasts and growing dimensions require independent analysis.

**[Exact higher-r null variance and planted covariance kernel](F0-isotropic-subspace-second-moment-boundaries.md):** an exact Gaussian-binomial null second moment for all r, and a correct general planted pair covariance using quotient exterior ranks. For every fixed r in the external sparse d<c regime, the planted mean shift is exponentially smaller than the null standard deviation, but this is *not* a TV statement, computational distinguisher theorem or hardness evidence. Higher-dimensional planted covariance does **not** admit the naive literal-intersection simplification; no new source hypothesis or Field-15 nomination.

**[Intermediate-r common-isotropic-subspace exact means](F0-isotropic-subspace-first-moment-hierarchy.md):** extends the merged decomposable-2-plane expectation to all `r<=d` under the full conditional-uniform planted/null alternating tensor laws. A Gaussian-binomial intersection sum gives exact means, and a fixed-q sparse-source bound places the relative mean difference at `q^(-Omega(n))` when `r<= (2-epsilon)(n-d)/m`. This is **not** TV indistinguishability, a polynomial counter or recovery algorithm. Earlier source likelihood ratio and null d-witness rarity credited to [#208 comment 6215551334](https://github.com/StanleyLl0yd/morph-kem/issues/208#issuecomment-6215551334).

## H-B: local-to-global compatibility with nontrivial overlapping patches

**Structural hypothesis, not a cryptographic assumption.** A public cover \(\mathcal U=\{U_i\}\) of a public carrier and a public finite alphabet define a global assignment x and compatible restricted local objects \(x\vert_{U_i}\). The sampler chooses an honest global x *first* and publishes polynomial-size local observations \(p_i=F_i(x\vert_{U_i})\), where each F_i is polynomial. A candidate y is accepted iff it is a global assignment and \(F_i(y\vert_{U_i})=p_i\) for **all** i. Thus the honest image is globally feasible by construction. Local feasibility is a necessary condition, never a hardness proof.

**Mature context / novelty obstacle:** [Abramsky-Brandenburger](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264) already study local-to-global sections and contextuality; [Bulatov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03021) and [Zhuk](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.01914) classify finite-language CSP tractability; public abelian consistency is often linear coboundary solving. The ordinary local-to-global obstruction, a fancy topology or a high nominal Betti number is NOT a new crypto foundation.

**Attack-first falsifiers:** (i) compute effective public marginal sections on overlaps and greedily glue; (ii) encode abelian transition values as a linear cochain/cocycle system and solve; (iii) exploit [F0-K3](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#5-f0-k3-local-constraints-on-a-public-low-width-primal-graph-have-a-public-section) on a public low-width primal graph or known decomposition; (iv) test finite-domain CSP algebraic tractability, SAT/SMT/ILP relaxations, [F0-K4 group-valued spanning-tree gauge](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#7-f0-k4-nonabelian-global-edge-relations-invert-even-at-maximal-treewidth) (which works even at maximal graph treewidth), and compactness; (v) derive a canonical public global object from local signatures. Genuine topological obstruction can coexist with an easy full global section on the *honest generated profiles*.

**Matched controls to preregister only if admitted:** same source and patch degrees with tree-like/low-width overlap, exact same local predicates with randomized public arrangement, and uncoupled independent patches *not falsely counted as honest global samples*. Compare original verifier acceptance, output support and witness fibre under matched bitlength.

**[F0-K9](F0-residual-core-and-acyclic-join.md#3-f0-k9-explicit-acyclic-hypergraph-joins-admit-a-polynomial-public-section)** also rejects exact gluing of polynomial-size **explicit extensional** tables over an acyclic hypergraph, even when primal treewidth is large; standard Yannakakis join-tree witness selection. **Status:** **HOLD**. No independently novel non-CSP/gluing relation or evidence about honest average-case inversion. It is a conceptual structural test, not a replacement for the already rejected topology family.

## H-C: history-sensitive noncommuting transformations with polynomial observations

**Structural hypothesis, not a cryptographic assumption.** Let X be a public finite or finitely presented family with polynomial encodings, and let \(T_1,\ldots,T_k\) be public polynomial-time partial transformations. The sampler selects an initial encoded x and a **polynomial-length** legal move sequence \(\sigma\) unconditionally from a fully specified polynomial sampler, computes a public polynomial-time observable \(P=F(x,\sigma)\) (possibly compressed endpoint and bounded intermediate observations). A candidate \((y,\tau)\), with \(\lvert\tau\rvert\le\mathrm{poly}(\lambda)\) and publicly checkable legality, passes iff \(F(y,\tau)=P\). **Any** candidate history is successful. The entire observable and witness encoding must be polynomial; putting an exponential state exploration into F or V fails immediately.

**Prior-art danger:** group actions, orbit/conjugacy, rewriting systems, Tietze/Andrews-Curtis, Pachner/Kirby transformations and dynamical CSP already occur in [MORPH-R0](../97-morph-r0-cross-line-postmortem.md) and [legacy cryptanalysis](../05-cryptanalysis.md). Noncommutativity or topological complexity alone has repeatedly failed the public quotient/normal-form gate. This is not currently claimed distinct from those subjects.

**Attack-first falsifiers:** (i) normalization of histories / public endpoint section and inverse moves, (ii) finite-quotient representations or action stabilizer recovery, (iii) sparse/short transcript synthesis by dynamic programming, meet-in-the-middle, SAT/MILP or group-word methods, (iv) bounded public state space or bounded-width transition factorization, (v) public group-valued edge transports allowing [F0-K4](F0-failure-mechanisms.md#7-f0-k4-nonabelian-global-edge-relations-invert-even-at-maximal-treewidth) even on highly connected noncommuting graphs, (vi) known mature action/word/reconfiguration formulation. Positive worst-case word-problem hardness alone is insufficient for the honest distribution. **No universal attack result** is claimed for arbitrary noncommuting transformations.

**Matched controls to preregister only if admitted:** commuting/linearized moves on the identical source, public normal-form controls, independent-history sources without planted conditioning; account for each move's actual bit/time cost.

**Status:** **HOLD / lower priority** because the entire mathematical category has extensive previous MORPH failure overlap. No positive Field 15, no candidate.
