## N1-57 (2026-10-14): phaseless rank-3 integer Pluecker data - arithmetic-matroid prior-art rejection, NOT a public polynomial inverter

**Research-only, no nomination.** Independent integer/algebraic-geometric inverse relation, intentionally preserving the **absolute-value signs ambiguity** that is absent from signed Pluecker coordinates. Unlike N1-56 symplectic GF(2), N1-55 ultrametric or N1-54 Hankel. The complete 15-field gate is screened before experiments. **Disposition: REJECT AS NEW CRYPTO MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION at Field 15; no claim that polynomial inversion is known for this exact restricted distribution.**

### MORPH-R0, all 15 fields

1. **PublicInstance:** n>=4 and the full **labeled** rank-3 maximal-minor absolute profile `P=(p_{ijk}=|det(A_i,A_j,A_k)|:1<=i<j<k<=n)`, `p_{123}=1`, each a bounded integer.
2. **Secret/Witness:** planted integer matrix `A=[I_3|Z]` of shape 3×n. Any candidate `B=[I_3|W]`, `W∈{-2,-1,0,1,2}^{3×(n-3)}`, with all same absolute 3×3 minors is accepted, regardless of planted signs and entries.
3. **Generation distribution:** each entry of Z iid *uniform* on {-2,-1,0,1,2}; anchors fixed I3. Compute all C(n,3) determinants exactly and take absolute values. No conditioning, resampling, missing rows or planted-solution filtering. Determinants are bounded by constants (e.g. Hadamard bound 3!·2³), so public length O(n³), source entropy 3(n-3)log2(5), and exact computation polynomial.
4. **Public verifier:** validate all n, labels, nonnegative p, `p_{123}=1`, check candidate B has exactly I3 first columns and specified bounded integer alphabet, recompute every |`det B_{ijk}`| exactly and compare all C(n,3) components. O(n³) arithmetic with constant-size input numbers.
5. **Attacker equivalent target:** `Equivalent(P)={B=[I3|W] with W bounded as above: forall i<j<k, |det B_ijk|=p_ijk}`. Any accepted B wins; planted Z not required.
6. **Kernel/fibre:** independently negating any **nonzero extra column** of B preserves every absolute determinant and anchor. Hence every honest input has at least 2^t accepted witnesses, t=#nonzero extra columns. More equivalences may exist and have NOT been counted. Zero columns contribute no sign bit.
7. **Known quotients/representations:** Grassmann/Pluecker maximal minors with erased orientation, oriented arithmetic matroids, absolute Pluecker data / phaseless determinant recovery, signed-Hermite normal form; studied **representation problems**, not a new hardness assumption. Importantly, the exact bounded/anchored restriction is a subproblem, not automatically equivalent in complexity to the full literature.
8. **Canonicalization/public attacks:** the 3 minors with **two anchor columns** per j reveal `|W_{1j}|,|W_{2j}|,|W_{3j}|` exactly (up to parity of column ordering, erased by abs). Write each nonzero coordinate as `s_{aj}|W_{aj}|`, sign s∈{±1}; all remaining minor comparisons reduce to a public finite Boolean sign-CSP. Normalize each nonzero extra column's first nonzero coordinate to **positive**, because whole-column sign flips do not change P. Thus at most **two unknown binary signs per extra column** remain, with all remaining constraints publicly evaluable. **This is a reduction, NOT a proven polynomial public section**. Worst-case sign search and average success have not been measured, and no hardness follows.
9. **Local/low-weight leakage:** each coordinate magnitude leaks from 2-anchor minors without solving any inverse problem. Smaller determinants with 1 or 0 anchor columns constrain cross-column signs and are a natural public SAT/ILP attack surface.
10. **Noise-to-optimization:** absolute-value sign erasure is part of the exact verifier, not a tunable noise parameter. Dropping minors, altering alphabet bounds, increasing rank, or changing anchor conditions defines a **different** relation and cannot rescue Field 15 automatically.
11. **Cover/base/fibre:** anchors I3 give a public chart in the ordinary Grassmannian. Whole-column sign flips define explicit accepted symmetries; no hidden bundle/lift is asserted. A full classification of fibres was NOT proved.
12. **Matched weak control:** only 2-anchor minors on the *same* Z provide coordinate magnitudes but not all cross-sign constraints. The full profile is mandatory for attacks. No empirical paired control or case count was run.
13. **Validation:** no source generation, scripts, samples, verifier trials, algorithm timings or numerical evidence are reported. Field 15 prior-art and an elementary public sign-CSP reduction already block nomination; do not initiate ad hoc parameter sweeps.
14. **Quantum screen:** a naive Grover search over at most 2(n-3) normalized sign bits is a possible generic bound, not a measured quantum attack, and not an OWF/PQ-security argument. Classical sign-CSP, signed normal form, matroid representation, exact enumeration of allowable signings and distributional fibre analysis precede any quantum claim.
15. **Positive novelty / why-not-mature: FAIL.** The full-rank basis determinant multiplicities are a slice of **representable torsion-free arithmetic matroids**; their representation and column sign equivalence are already studied. See [Pagaria & Paolini, *Representations of torsion-free arithmetic matroids*, European J. Combin. 93 (2021), 103272](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2020.103272) and [Pagaria's thesis on arithmetic matroid orientations/Pluecker data](https://www.dm.unibo.it/~roberto.pagaria/Thesis_phd.pdf), plus [Pluecker representations of Grassmannians](https://doi.org/10.1007/s44426-025-00007-x). Neither a bounded 5-symbol alphabet nor absolute values establish an independent new mathematical assumption. Specific anchored fixed-rank average-case distribution is **not** asserted to have a literature-equivalent time bound; Field15 is rejected for shared mathematical problem/representation, not for a conjectured easy algorithm.
