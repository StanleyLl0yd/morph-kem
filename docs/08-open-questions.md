## Before proposing a new family

1. What *mathematically independent* public relation and distribution are being specified, and why is it not de Bruijn assembly, projective frame normalization, exact electrical-resistance/Laplacian inversion, finite-field Hankel/linear-system realization, p-adic ultrametric digit-trie reconstruction, bounded-width counting DP, a finite-quotient normal form, matching/flow/rank, generic planted CSP or a relabeled mature crypto assumption? Provide positive, falsifiable **Field-15** evidence with references.
2. Can `PublicGen` and `Verify` be performed in polynomial time on honest instances, with explicit public-bitlength, entropy-bearing random source and unconditional distribution? Does generation sneak in exponential conditioning?
3. Can the verifier's **entire** accepted fibre `Equivalent(P)={candidate:Verify(P,candidate)}` be characterized? Does public canonicalization immediately produce a representative?
4. Which public algebraic, combinatorial, spectrum, finite-quotient, lattice, geometric, graph-width, interpolation, SAT/SMT, ILP and dynamic-programming attacks apply **before** toy-scale experiments?
5. What independent source distribution, negative control, complete predeclared enumeration horizon, budget and original-verifier cross-check would actually falsify the new claim?
6. Does the proposed relation admit meaningful quantum search, hidden-shift, hidden-subgroup or other accelerations after the best known classical reduction?
7. How can independent reviewers reproduce reported issue-only research? N1-46..49 and N1-51 bundles were not committed; do not represent this repository as containing their full experiments.
