# 08 — Open questions

## After M4

M4 shows that a closed genuinely 2D surface with zero free collapses can still have an elementary global equivalent-witness construction.

The next research questions are:

- What irregular non-manifold 2-complex distribution avoids a hand-constructible primal/dual witness?
- Can a good planted acyclic matching be embedded without producing role-dependent incidence statistics?
- Can the target vector avoid being implied by an easily recognizable global class?
- Does the witness relation admit a compact SAT/CSP formulation that solves toy instances immediately?
- Which Hasse-graph width parameters control exact search?
- How many equivalent witnesses exist and how does that multiplicity affect any possible one-way relation?
- Can a protected secret be invariant across equivalent witnesses without becoming public?
- Is higher ambient dimension required, or can a sufficiently irregular 2-complex work?
- What quantum search/walk model is appropriate once a candidate distribution survives classical attacks?

## M5 gate

Before scaling an M5 distribution, test:

1. free and near-free reductions;
2. primal/dual tree, forest, matching, and matroid-style constructions;
3. generic greedy acyclic matching;
4. bounded branch-and-bound;
5. SAT/CSP;
6. incidence-role distinguishers;
7. canonicalization and width structure.

No KEM wrapper until a primitive survives these gates.
